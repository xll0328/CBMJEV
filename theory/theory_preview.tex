\begin{abstract}
This appendix records finite-scope proofs, counterexamples, and implementation
assumptions for a proposed study of adaptive concept measurement. It reports
no real-data experiments and makes no claim of novel general acquisition or
calibration theory. The accompanying research package separates proved
statements under explicit assumptions from empirical conditions that remain
to be checked on public benchmarks.
\end{abstract}
